US shopping perspective

Buying from the United States?

For US shoppers, international shipping costs, customs rules and product restrictions can substantially affect the final purchase decision.

  • Compare shipping options using your destination ZIP code.
  • Check current US import rules and applicable charges.
  • Keep invoices and accurate item descriptions.
  • Review carrier restrictions before shipping fragrances or liquids.
European shopping perspective

Ordering to Europe?

EU buyers should account for VAT, customs procedures, shipping coverage and local consumer protections when evaluating cross-border purchases.

  • Check VAT treatment and potential handling fees.
  • Confirm whether taxes are collected at checkout.
  • Review local product compliance requirements.
  • Compare tracked shipping services and insurance terms.

03 / Quality control

Kakobuy QC Photos: What Should You Inspect?

QC images help you examine visible product details before international shipment, but they cannot verify every property.

Read the guide

Look for uneven stitching, loose threads, asymmetrical shapes, visible stains, incorrect colors and differences from the product description. Request measurement photos where available.

Lighting and camera angles can distort colors. QC photographs cannot reliably establish material composition, long-term durability or authenticity.

  • Check front, back and close-up views.
  • Compare measurements.
  • Flag visible issues before shipment.
04 / Shipping

Kakobuy Shipping Cost: How to Estimate Your Budget

International shipping depends on destination, parcel dimensions, weight, carrier and available service lines.

Read the guide

Calculate the combined weight of your products and packaging. Some carriers bill using the greater of actual and volumetric weight. A common volumetric formula uses length × width × height divided by a carrier-specific divisor.

Include handling fees, insurance and potential import charges. Use a current provider quote instead of relying on old haul examples.

  • Estimate package dimensions.
  • Compare available carrier quotes.
  • Reserve a buffer for unexpected charges.

05 / Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

Direct answers to the most common Kakobuy spreadsheet questions.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated list of product references, category information and links used to discover items and compare options. The information should be independently verified before ordering.

How do I use Kakobuy spreadsheet links?

Choose a category, open a listing, review specifications and photos, compare alternatives and verify availability, fees and shipping details before purchasing.

How can I estimate Kakobuy shipping?

Consider actual and volumetric parcel weight, destination, shipping line, handling fees and potential import charges. Request a current quote from the service provider.

What should I check in QC photos?

Inspect shape, stitching, labels, measurements, color, visible defects and differences from the listing. QC photographs are not proof of authenticity or long-term quality.
